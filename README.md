# Living My Vows

A short reflection on prayer, chastity, poverty and obedience in community life, prepared for a day of recollection for sisters preparing to renew their vows.

Prepared by Fr. Solomon Nirmalan, CMF.

## About the tool

Each sister meets sixteen situations from everyday convent and mission life, four in each area: prayer and life, chastity, poverty and obedience. She chooses the answer closest to how she really lives, or "None of these describes me" with a few words of her own. At the end she sees which area is a gift to thank God for and which is an invitation to grow, with a Scripture passage, a question for prayer and small practical steps, and she writes her own renewal intention.

The tool is a mirror for personal prayer, not a validated measurement instrument. Results should not be used to compare or evaluate sisters.

The whole tool is one file, `index.html`. It runs in any phone or computer browser. Answers stay on each person's own device and are never sent anywhere.
